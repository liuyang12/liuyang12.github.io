Course overview

This course develops the foundations and practical skills for turning data into reliable insights and predictive models. It combines mathematical intuition, statistical reasoning, Python demonstrations, and applications. Foundation models and AI agents support the workflow throughout, while students learn to verify generated code, assess evidence, and explain their decisions.

Course structure

Lectures Focus Topics
L1–L4 Foundations The data science workflow, linear algebra, probability and statistics, applications, data quality, and preprocessing.
L5–L7 Learning from data Regression, classification, unsupervised learning, and embeddings, with emphasis on evaluation and generalization.
L8–L9 Modern AI Deep learning, foundation models, LLMs, and agentic systems for interactive data science applications.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

  • Formulate a data science problem and build a reproducible analysis workflow.
  • Prepare and visualize data, and interpret uncertainty and statistical evidence.
  • Select, fit, and evaluate supervised and unsupervised models on unseen data.
  • Explain learned representations and the core mechanisms of deep learning and foundation models.
  • Use AI agents effectively, verify their outputs, and communicate and defend the results.

Course calendar

Nine lectures, one midterm, and two project-presentation sessions.

Week Date Session Topic and focus
1 Sep 2 L1 Introduction to Data Science and Foundation Models
Data science workflows, data collection, exploratory analysis, visualization, AI prompting, and verification.
2 Sep 9 L2 Linear Algebra for Data Science
Data matrices, similarity, covariance, eigenvectors, principal component analysis, and explained variance.
3 Sep 16 L3 Probability and Statistics for Data Science
Distributions, conditional probability, Bayes’ rule, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, and p-values.
4 Sep 23 L4 Data Science Applications and Data Wrangling
Problem formulation, data quality, missing data, cleaning, feature encoding, scaling, and reproducible preprocessing.
5 Sep 30 L5 Regression: Predicting Continuous Outcomes
Linear regression, coefficient interpretation, model evaluation, overfitting, the bias–variance tradeoff, and regularization.
6 Oct 7 L6 Classification: Predicting Categories
Logistic regression, decision trees, thresholds, precision and recall, ROC-AUC, and class imbalance.
7 Oct 14 — Reading week
No scheduled lecture.
8 Oct 21 Exam Midterm exam
In class; closed book.
9 Oct 28 L7 Unsupervised Learning and Embeddings
Polynomial features, k-means clustering, learned embeddings, cosine similarity, semantic search, and transfer.
10 Nov 4 L8 Deep Learning
Neural networks and function approximation, backpropagation, automatic differentiation, optimizers, CNNs, and ResNet.
11 Nov 11 L9 Foundation Models and Agentic Data Science (planned)
Attention, pretraining, LLMs, and tool-using agents for data science workflows, with verification and evaluation.
12 Nov 18 P1 Group project presentations
First presentation session; approximately 10 minutes per group.
13 Nov 25 P2 Group project presentations
Second presentation session; approximately 10 minutes per group.
